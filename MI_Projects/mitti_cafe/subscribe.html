The Concept

A plant in our farm.
Yours forever.

Adopt one (or more) plants in our hydroponic towers. We grow, harvest, and pack your monthly box — delivered to your door across Jaipur.

How it works

Simple as planting a seed

Four easy steps from our farm to your table.

🌿 Step 1

Choose & Subscribe

Pick a plant variety and subscribe. Name your plant if you like.

💧 Step 2

We Nurture It

Your plant lives in our hydroponic tower. We water, nourish, and monitor it daily.

📦 Step 3

Monthly Harvest Box

We harvest your plant monthly and deliver a curated box to your door.

🏷️ Bonus

Your Name on the Farm

A tag on the farm tower bears your name. Show your friends.
